1 player · Racing · Ages 5+

Scribble Speedway

Draw a racetrack with your finger and the game turns your scribble into a real circuit — smoothing the wobbles, rounding off the corners, nudging the tarmac clear of the trees — and then you race it. Eighteen challenges across four chapters, each one a field of ponds, boulders and golden rings to design a circuit through, with rivals waiting once you've drawn it. The corners are the whole game: the outside of a bend can be taken faster and the inside covers less ground, so there's a racing line to find on a track nobody has ever driven. Auto-brake for smaller drivers, coins for the garage, and a Daily Circuit where the whole family gets the same field and each draws their own way round it. Save your favourites to the Track Book and your best lap becomes a ghost car for everyone else to chase.

Ricochet Spire

Slay the Spire meets Breakout. Bounce a ball, break some bricks — then start drafting cards, and watch it turn into something else. Fireball, Explosive Ball and Chain Lightning together detonate the whole screen; Mirror World reverses your controls every thirty seconds; Gravity Shift keeps deciding which way is down. Pick your own route up a branching map of battles, elites, shops, campfires and bosses, with health that carries the whole climb. Death ends the run, but the shards come home. There's a Daily Challenge too — one seed a day, the same spire for everybody.

Rocket Rescue

There are little creatures stranded down in the caves and you have the only ship small enough to get to them. Drag anywhere to fly. The rock is the real enemy — the asteroids, spikes, ion gates and comets are just the punctuation. Nothing ever sits on the line you were already flying, so every rescue is a decision about how far off the safe route you'll go. Twenty caves across four worlds, each ending somewhere a Guardian lives, plus a tractor beam, a slow field and a stardust shop to spend your rescues in. Then there's Deep Rescue: one endless cave a day, the same one for everybody, closing in until it beats you.
